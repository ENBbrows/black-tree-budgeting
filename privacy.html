Privacy & Security

A plain-language explanation of what protects your numbers, and who — if anyone — can see them.

🔒 Your data is private to you by default

Every income entry, expense, goal, debt, and bill you log is tied to your account and enforced at the database level — not just hidden in the app's screens. No other client, and no unassigned advisor, can read or change your numbers. This is the same rule for every single account, with no exceptions built in.

🤝 If you work with a Black Tree advisor

Your advisor can see your numbers — read-only, never editable — so they can prepare for and guide your consultations. They only ever see the clients they specifically work with, never a directory of everyone using the app, and never another advisor's clients. Advisor access is something Black Tree Investments sets up on our side; it isn't something any account can grant itself.

📷 Scanned bills are never stored

When you snap a photo of a bill or receipt, it's read directly on your own device — it is never uploaded to any server. The moment the amount, date, and category are pulled out for you to confirm, the image is discarded. We never keep a copy anywhere.

📴 Works offline — so a copy also lives on your device

To work without a signal, your most recent numbers are kept on whichever phone or computer you're using, and sync back up once you're online. On a device you share with others, log out when you're done so that cached copy doesn't stay sitting there for the next person.

✉️ Signing in never uses a password

You sign in with a one-time link sent to your email — nothing to type, remember, or accidentally reuse elsewhere. Each link is single-use, expires quickly, and only works for the exact address it was sent to, so forwarding one doesn't hand anyone else access.

🏢 Who operates this

Black Tree Investments runs and hosts this app. It's built so that day-to-day use never exposes one client's information to another, and access to the systems behind it is limited to what's needed to keep the service running and support you if something goes wrong.

Questions about your data? Reach us at info@blacktreeinvestments.com.
