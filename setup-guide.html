Get set up in 2 minutes

Three shortcuts, three purposes: log income, log expenses, and view your full dashboard. Each one installs as its own icon.

📱 iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open income.html, expense.html or dashboard.html in Safari (not Chrome — this only works in Safari on iOS).
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow) at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Confirm the name and tap Add.
  5. Repeat for each of the other two pages you want as a shortcut.

🤖 Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the page you want in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  4. Confirm the name and tap Add.

🖥️ Desktop (Dashboard)

  1. Open dashboard.html in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar (or menu → Install Black Tree — Financial Dashboard).
  3. It now opens as its own app window from your desktop/taskbar — no browser tabs.

Works without signal

Once you've opened a page while online, it stays on your device — logging income or an expense with no signal saves instantly and quietly syncs the next time you're connected. The very first sign-in link does need a connection to arrive by email.

Your data stays yours

Sign-in links are single-use, expire quickly, and only work for the email address they were sent to — so forwarding one doesn't hand anyone your numbers. Each shortcut just opens a locked door; without your own sign-in link, whoever taps it sees a login screen, not your data.
